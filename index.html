Type a sentence and DistilGPT-2, running entirely in your browser, scores every token. Those scores combine into a single number called perplexity.

Token

A small piece of text, often a word or part of one. Models read tokens, not letters.

Probability

How likely the model thought a token was, given everything before it. Always between 0 and 1.

Perplexity

The average surprise across all tokens. Lower means the model found the text more predictable.

Sentence lab

The model reads your sentence left to right and asks, for each token: how likely was this, given what came before?

The model downloads once, on your first calculation.

Token probability analysis
